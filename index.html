The Archive

At the close of each month the Museum takes a snapshot of every site’s results and keeps it here for good. If a site’s rules ever change, its earlier history can be read from the archive instead of being recomputed. The conservators check each archived month against the universe as it computes today, so any month whose history has changed is caught.

Catalogue a month by hand

The archive fills itself every night. If it ever stops, download a month here, add it to the archive folder of the Museum repo, and add the month to archive/index.json.
